### 10. A2A Protocol — 1-year milestone, 150+ orgs, Linux Foundation

### 11. MemPalace viral launch (April 6) — convergent project, SQLite+vector memory

**Source:** https://github.com/milla-jovovich/mempalace · https://nicholasrhodes.substack.com/p/mempalace-ai-memory-review-benchmarks
