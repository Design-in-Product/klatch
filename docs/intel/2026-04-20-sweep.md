### 6. Vite 8 — Klatch is on v6, two major versions behind
Vite 8.0 (March 12) replaced Rollup+esbuild with Rolldown (Rust-based). Breaking changes: `build.rollupOptions` → `build.rolldownOptions`; Lightning CSS replaces esbuild CSS minification; Oxc replaces esbuild for JS transforms. Claims 10-30x faster builds. Vite 7 is the intermediate step.
**Source:** https://vite.dev/blog/announcing-vite8
**Klatch impact:** Klatch is on `vite: "^6.0.0"` (confirmed in package.json). Migration requires two hops (6→7→8) or direct 6→8 with the compat layer. Not urgent but accumulating technical debt. Add to dep maintenance backlog with medium priority. Iris's dev loop (Tailwind @source) from the Tailwind v4.1 finding also benefits from Vite 8's speed improvement.

### 7. Gemma 4 local model — validates Argus's adoption plan

**Source:** https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemma-4/
**Klatch impact:** Confirms Argus's local model viability research (Gemma 4 / Qwen 3 noted in COORDINATION.md Round 26). The 26B MoE variant is the right target for cross-vendor entity channels vision. No action needed yet; validates existing research direction.
