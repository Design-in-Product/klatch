## HIGH RELEVANCE

### 1. Claude Fable 5 + Mythos 5 Suspended by US Export-Control Directive (June 12)

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 (a new tier above Opus) in early June. On June 12, 2026 — three days after GA — at 5:21 PM ET, Anthropic received a US government directive under national-security export-control authority and shut down all access to both models globally. The scope: foreign users, foreign nationals inside the US, and even foreign-national Anthropic employees — making a clean nationality-gated deployment infeasible. Anthropic suspended the models for all customers to achieve compliance. Fallback: all other models (including Opus 4.8) remain unaffected. This is the first documented government-forced takedown of a publicly deployed frontier model.

**Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access / https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-disables-access-to-fable-5-and-mythos-5-to-comply-with-government-directive.html
**Klatch impact:** Neither `claude-fable-5` nor any Mythos 5 ID appears in `AVAILABLE_MODELS` (`packages/shared/src/types.ts:1-6`). No immediate code change needed. Strategic notes: (a) Anthropic's recommended fallback from the suspension is Opus 4.8 — Klatch still has Opus 4.7 as its ceiling; adding Opus 4.8 is now higher-priority. (b) This event is the strongest evidence yet for the cross-vendor moat argument (Roadmap "Cross-vendor entity channels") — a single-vendor dependency on Anthropic now carries demonstrated regulatory closure risk. (c) Vendor-risk profile elevated beyond IPO volatility concerns: governments can force model takedowns with days of notice.
**Prior mentions:** "Fable" appears once in `docs/mail/read/memo-janus-memory-research-synthesis-2026-04-12.md` but is unrelated (lowercase, different context).
