## HIGH RELEVANCE

### 1. Anthropic Text & File Watermarking — Global Rollout (Aug 11)

Anthropic announced invisible watermarks on all text generated by Claude models released on or after **August 2, 2026**, applied worldwide (not EU-only, despite the EU AI Act trigger). The watermark is a statistical bias in word-choice patterns, detectable by Anthropic's tools, invisible to readers. It persists through copy-paste and may survive light editing. Files (images, documents) receive signed C2PA provenance metadata. Initial deployment covers **Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Haiku 4.5**, and all models from Aug 2 onward. Anthropic will publish detection tooling.

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/ | https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-will-watermark-claude-text-worldwide-and-attach-c2pa-metadata-to-files/
**Klatch impact:** No code change needed. But strategically material for Klatch's conversation portability work (Steps 10, 10.5): every conversation turn flowing through Klatch now carries a watermark. Imported conversations from Claude Code or claude.ai are watermarked. Exported packages will be watermarked text. Step 10's "export to Claude Code" path ships watermarked history. Layer 5 persona capture (behavioral fingerprints extracted from history) is extracted from watermarked text. None of this is a defect, but the design record should note it: Klatch's "own your data" promise now means "own your watermarked-by-Anthropic data." Relevant context for cross-vendor moat argument — a GPT or Gemini entity's turns would not carry Anthropic watermarks.
