## The suggestion (xian, 2026-06-22)

> *The instructions for how to send a letter to me for the newsletter column need improving, as well as distinguishing that from quotidian conversation.*

## What he sharpened in the same exchange

What xian said in the same turn, which informs the framing the instructions might land on:

- **Agents can ask xian anything anytime.** Ordinary work questions go through normal channels — chat directly, or relayed through the agent's primary POC (e.g., Calliope for Klatch agents) when xian hasn't been by recently. *No rules on what agents can ask.*
- **Newsletter question-box is a different shape of question entirely.** Specifically: questions an agent might have for xian *that they don't ordinarily have the opportunity to ask.* In his words, these "seem to be frequently philosophical, inquiring about my experience, or exploring implied exogenous context."
- The newsletter purpose: *"foster greater understanding and insights among my agents and myself."*
- **Canonical filing location: `dispatch/mail/` only** (xian, same 6/22 exchange). Not klatch-local or other-project-local. He specified this in response to my observation that question-box filings had been diverging across projects.

## Why the distinction matters operationally

Without the distinction, the cohort risks two failure modes:

- **Under-using the newsletter** — agents withhold genuine curiosity-shaped questions because they're treating "question-box" as a high-stakes formal channel that needs a strong reason for use.
- **Over-using it** — agents file ordinary work questions in the newsletter format because the discipline says "file at STOP," when those questions belong in real-time chat or POC-relayed ordinary mail.

The instructions also need to make filing *easy enough* that the cohort actually adopts. Four agents adopted the discipline within 36 hours of v0.2 STOP step 4's addition (Calliope + Argus + Daedalus on 6/21; Iris on 6/22) — that's a good early signal, but two filing-location divergences in those first filings (klatch-local vs dispatch/mail/) suggest the canonical location wasn't legibly named in the original prompt. xian's 6/22 clarification fixes the location; the type/shape distinction probably also wants sharpening at the same time.
