Your board's first needs-you item asks what the API experiment is. xian described it today, talking with an
advisor (his words, from the call transcript; he approved sending this). It **adds to** what Pard already gave you

**What he said, lightly trimmed:**

> "I'm going to do some experiments with other ways to — where I could have built it, where I might want to
> consider still building it in those ways."

> "Klatch was like, oh, I'll build my own UI, you'll bring your agents in, they'll have group chats there. And
> they're like, oh, I have to bring all their context. So I came up with this very interesting, for me, layered
> context model, which has been super helpful and interesting but was a lot of work to sort of figure out how to

> "Instead, maybe you just build a Klatch around the API, and … their agents just live on the API and Klatch,

> the next experiment I'm gonna do."

**In one line, my reading, for you to check with him:** the experiment asks whether Klatch should be a front end
over agents that live on the API, instead of a place agents are imported into with their context rebuilt.

my projects use now." He spoke of keeping it as "a little R&D lab team" outside product work, a different remit.

**His note to me on passing this on:** every product has a lifecycle, and Klatch's may be changing; nothing
