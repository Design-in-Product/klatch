## What happened today

xian and I finished the Theme 3 interview (questions 7-10: what Klatch is becoming). The headline findings:

**Step 10 is 1.0.** xian identified the roundtrip — import, do structured work, export with context — as the 1.0 value proposition. Users never have to burn their ships. They pass through Klatch on the way to somewhere else. The roundtrip wasn't originally planned; it was discovered as the import work revealed that context could be carried back out. This is Gall's Law applied to product strategy — the complex system (context interchange protocol) evolved from the simple one that worked (a group chat for AI agents).

**Phase 3 (export UI) is the most significant UX challenge of the project.** xian described it as "fluid, supportive, service design-oriented — something with the qualities of an LLM under the hood with the comfort of a UI." He explicitly gave me creative license: "room to play and think and explore and do some genuinely innovative work."

**Workflows are the most relevant futuristic feature.** A Klatch channel is a persistent, repeatable multi-agent workflow — like a skill, except one involving multiple agents. The three interaction modes are building blocks within workflows, not the taxonomy. Added to the evaluation skeleton as a cross-cutting concern.

**The blog is not a contract.** xian shares insights because they're genuinely interesting and valuable — not as product promises. The blog and the product serve different purposes. The evaluation measures against canonical use cases, not blog explorations.

## The design principles synthesis

The second half of the session was a synthesis of design principles that have emerged across all three sessions. We identified four clusters under a meta-principle ("who bears the burden?"): absorb complexity, communicate with clarity, preserve human agency, build from evidence. Plus five developing areas where xian has instincts but not yet codified positions (visual language, error/recovery, delight/personality, density, agent experience).

The result is a new living document at `docs/ux/design-principles.md`. It's meant to support consistency without imposing doctrine — a reference for me, Daedalus, and future contributors.
