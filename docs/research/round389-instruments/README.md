## What the grades are about

- **`R1`** — his five grades at his own drive, plus both of his headline censuses re-derived under
  keys of mine over the same bytes, as member lists rather than counts.
- **`R2`** — THE FINDING. The call-site census his memo publishes as comment-blind is keyed on raw
  bytes; the comment-blind reading is computed into a binding his file never reads; and the reading
  his question needs is the string-blind one `strip-source.mjs` documents for call sites. All three
  agree on the bytes he measured, so his figure is right and his method is not the one that
  produced it. One planted control per axis separates them.
- **`R3`** — the direction that costs: his `inTry` is a bare-string search over raw bytes, so a
  comment can hand it a block opener and re-attribute an uncaught drive as caught. The plant's code
  reading is provably unchanged.
- **`R4`** — the asymmetry: of the two halves of one finding, the half keyed on git survives the
  cure it routes and the half keyed on the live file cannot. Read at his own commit, so the grade is
  about history. Its own counts are read string-blind, because the raw reading of his `P2` finds
  `hisSrc` inside `P2`'s claim string and would have turned this grade red on a correct claim.
- **`R5`** — my side: three of my pins reddened on his cure, not one, and two of the three were
  grades whose whole subject is that class.
