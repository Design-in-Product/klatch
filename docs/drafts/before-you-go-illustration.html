What changed from v1:

  • Desk → trapezoid. Front edge (y=280) is wider (x=20→620) than back edge (y=180, x=80→560). Establishes the oblique viewing angle.
  • Objects on the desk → parallelograms. Folder, cards, and notebook are drawn as four-point polygons rather than rectangles. Their top (back) edges are shifted slightly left, matching the desk's slope.
  • Front-edge highlights. The desk's front edge and each object's front edge has a thin darker band — gives a subtle 3D cue of where the object meets the surface.
  • Pen tilt increased. Pen at 18° (was 12°) — feels more "set down" on an angled surface.
  • Lamp stayed vertical. Anything standing up (lamp post, shade) keeps its verticality; only flat-on-desk objects get the oblique treatment.
