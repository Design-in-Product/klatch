Bringing Conversations Into a Room — illustration preview

Draft v1 — 2026-05-28. Same calm slate vocabulary + oblique perspective as Before You Go.

Existing conversations, brought into one room. One has earned a role (the red mark). One is just being set down.

The idea it carries: a klatch isn't a fresh group chat configured from scratch — it's existing conversations (each with its own history and identity) composed into a shared table. The tilted card is the composition gesture in motion (bringing a conversation in). The red-marked card is a role: a conversation promoted into persistent identity. The cards stay distinct even at the shared table — a quiet nod to "klatches are synthetic" (each agent keeps its own conversation; the room is the human's view).

Tweakable: card count/arrangement; whether to add a faint "human's-eye" framing vs. keep it neutral; tab colors (currently slate variants to stay calm — could differentiate more); the tilted-card angle; whether the table reads enough as "round/shared" vs. "desk."
