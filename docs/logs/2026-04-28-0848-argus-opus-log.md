### Coverage by scope item

**1. Round-trip fidelity matrix (11 sub-tests):**
Parameterized matrix across (±project, entity count 1/2/5, ±channel files, ±project files, ±compaction, ±reflections-with-mixed-ingress, ±non-empty L4) — 10 cases via `it.each()`. Plus a forked-round-trip case asserting source-instance preservation under forceImport. Each case proves export → fresh-import → re-export produces structurally equivalent manifests on identity, content, and layer sidecars.

**Key adjustment from initial run:** initial test required full manifest equality on round-trip including provenance. Re-export of an imported channel advances the provenance chain by design (the new export becomes a new hop, and `layer_fidelity.L1` flips from 'absent' on native channels to 'full' on imported channels because `channel.source !== 'native'` after import). Both are intentional — not regressions. Test relaxed to mask provenance + per-hop layer_fidelity from the equality, with a separate `chain growth on re-export` test pinning the +1-hop behavior explicitly. Identity fields, layer sidecars, and content remain load-bearing.

**Incidental finding (not a Round 31b blocker, flagged for Daedalus):** in `package-builder.ts:58`, the original-source provenance entry summary is constructed as `\`Original ${channel.source === 'claude-code' ?
