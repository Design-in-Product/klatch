## 14:45 — AuditBench Methodology Review (Calliope assignment #3)

Written to `docs/research/auditbench-methodology-review.md`. Key findings for Theseus/MAXT:

1. **Scaffolded probing > fixed quizzes.** AuditBench's most effective detection used AI-generated diverse probes, not fixed questions. Quiz v4 should evolve toward scaffolded probes per-session.

2. **Behavioral manifestation > self-report.** Testing whether the model *acts on* context (behavioral probes) is more reliable than asking if it *remembers* context (recall questions). Directly addresses Confabulated vs. Correct distinction.

AuditBench uses fine-tuning (not context injection) to implant behaviors, then adversarial training to resist confession. Structurally parallel to AXT but much stronger intervention — our context injection is weaker, making confabulation (false positives) our bigger risk vs. their missed detections (false negatives).
