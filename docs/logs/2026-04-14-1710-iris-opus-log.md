## 17:30 — UX walkthrough begins: Iris's five topics

Presented five topics from my evaluation, discussed one at a time (inchworm process — depth on each, track it, move on).

### Topic 1: The visibility gap — CONFIRMED
The backend computes rich context (prompt-debug returns per-layer status with source, size, file counts). The UI shows colored dots. Not a feature gap — a presentation gap. The data exists; the UI doesn't show it. Closes incrementally. xian agreed immediately.

xian's addition: this is part of the design exploration pass — develop the holistic design vision first (mental models, IA, mobile, gestures), then the specific interface decisions flow from that vision.

### Topic 2: Sidebar IA — HEARD
Three jobs (navigation, communication, actions) with uneven success. The sidebar currently communicates "chat app with projects" rather than the distinctive Klatch concepts (entities, modes, workflows). Footer grab bag. Entity existence invisible from sidebar.

xian's note: Claude Desktop just unified their three surfaces and their sidebar is closer to what he originally envisioned. Worth researching as a pattern source and directional signal. His own observations will connect to the entity visibility point.

### Topic 3: Conversation experience — HEARD
Empty state doesn't reflect channel purpose (no entities, mode, pinned files visible). Multi-entity rendering needs live observation — I've only evaluated from code review.

xian's note: he has specific experiences to share. Claude Desktop now has a dashboard/front-page concept worth considering. Also noted the irony of a dashboard you can't navigate back to — itself a wayfinding finding.

### Topic 4: Entity management — HEARD + MAJOR REFRAME FROM XIAN
My observation: entity creation at workflow scale is tedious, no team view, no channel-to-entity relationship visible.

**xian's reframe (the most important insight of the session):** Entities aren't abstractions you define in a manager — they're existing conversations that you promote into a role. Each PM leadership role already lives as an ongoing chat. When you want a klatch, you bring existing conversations into a room together. The klatch is a meeting of existing chats, not a new conversation with pre-configured personas. The entity IS its conversation, given a seat at a shared table.

This inverts the current mental model: current UI says "create entity → assign to channel." The actual workflow is "have a conversation (entity develops through use) → bring it into a klatch (it joins a meeting with full context)."

From-scratch creation exists but isn't the central use case. xian will describe the intended workflow and actual experience when it's his turn.

### Topic 5: Import-to-export arc — HEARD, RESONATED
The roundtrip (import → work → export) has no experiential thread connecting its three acts. Each act is a separate interaction. The transition from Act 1 (import) to Act 2 (work in a klatch) — "I've imported these conversations, now I want to use them together" — is the gap illuminated by the entity reframe from topic 4.

xian: "I think I like this one the most so far because it names something I had not fully attended to yet." His restatement: just enough context to understand what they're doing, just enough progressive reveal to avoid overwhelm, power users can skip straight to action. Three-level design challenge.
